# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Last Updated:** [Insert Date]

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between Lucida ("Data Processor") and the Customer ("Data Controller") using the Lucida API and ML scoring services (the "Service").

## 1. Definitions

- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that is processed by the Data Processor on behalf of the Data Controller in the course of providing the Service (e.g., Lead email addresses, contact details).
- **"Data Controller"** means the Customer who determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data.
- **"Data Processor"** means Lucida, which processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
- **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Data Processor to assist in fulfilling its obligations with respect to providing the Service.

## 2. Processing of Personal Data

### 2.1 Scope and Purpose
The Processor will process Personal Data solely for the purpose of providing the Service, specifically calculating predictive lead scores and adapting the machine learning models to the Controller’s historical conversion feedback.

### 2.2 Controller Instructions
The Processor shall only process Personal Data on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required to do so by applicable law. Uploading data to the API constitutes documented instructions.

### 2.3 Nature of Data
The Controller may submit Personal Data to the Service, the extent of which is determined and controlled by the Controller, and which may include, but is not limited to: names, email addresses, job titles, and engagement metrics ("Lead Data").

## 3. Sub-processing

The Controller grants the Processor general authorization to engage Sub-processors. The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of Sub-processors.

Current Sub-processors include:
- **Hosting Providers:** (e.g., Railway, AWS) for infrastructure and computing power.
- **Authentication:** Clerk, for managing Dashboard access.
- **Billing:** Dodo Payments, for processing SaaS subscriptions.

## 4. Security Measures

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
- Encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.
- Logical isolation of tenant data within the platform.
- Regular vulnerability scanning and security reviews.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller's obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights (e.g., right to access, deletion).

## 6. Deletion or Return of Data

Upon termination of the Services or upon the Controller's written request, the Processor shall delete all Personal Data processed on behalf of the Controller, unless applicable laws require storage of the Personal Data.

## 7. Audits and Inspections

The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in this DPA.

## 8. Liability

The liability of each party under this DPA shall be subject to the exclusions and limitations of liability set out in the Terms of Service.
